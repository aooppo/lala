# Research: Coffee Table Four-Task Redesign

## Shot decomposition

**Decision**: Use four equal five-second tasks with explicit start and terminal states.
**Rationale**: This matches the requested storyboard and creates a human gate before every future paid task.
**Alternatives considered**: One blind 20-second generation and four tasks launched together were rejected for continuity and paid-call risk.

## TASK-03 treatment

**Decision**: Keep the beauty shot inside the established living room and preserve spatial landmarks.
**Rationale**: A product-only white PDP breaks the requested lifestyle story and room continuity.
**Alternatives considered**: Reusing the earlier isolated cutaway was rejected.

## Future Live sequencing

**Decision**: Generate and review strictly in task order, binding each accepted terminal frame into the next task's source plan.
**Rationale**: Product/prop/character continuity is easier to reject early and prevents blind spend.
**Alternatives considered**: Automatic retry or replacement after a task ID exists is prohibited.
